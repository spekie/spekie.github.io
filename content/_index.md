# About Me

My name is German, but I am mostly known as Spekie on the internet. I am from Saint Petersburg, Russia.

My primary interests are in computer architecture, cybersecurity, system administration, and digital privacy.

I have many computers, with the primary operating systems running on them include [NixOS](https://nixos.org), [Gentoo Linux](https://www.gentoo.org), [Void Linux](https://voidlinux.org), and BSD derivatives such as [OpenBSD](https://www.openbsd.org) and [NetBSD](https://www.netbsd.org)

My projects can be found on [GitHub](https://github.com/spekie). I usually program in C and C++ but I am currently learning more programming languages.

As for human languages, I know Russian, English, German, Romanian, Polish, and currently learning Japanese.
