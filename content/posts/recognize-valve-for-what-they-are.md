## Introduction

The title is simple. This will be a short first post from me to call out everyone for fantasizing about this company named Valve Corporation the developers of the digital distribution platform used by nearly everyone called Steam. I am simply going to ask of people to recognize Valve for what they are and they are a corporation not your "friend". Many people think they are some sort of divine thing that happened to save us from the likes of Microsoft by investing into Linux infrastructure and projects. While I will admit they are putting quite a lot of money into Linux projects, this is not because they have a good heart or believe in the open-source ecosystem. They can say whatever they want and preach about open-source they do not care about it, it is simply a business decision.

## Alternative

If they truly cared about open-source and our rights, we would actually own our games and not have Digital Rights Management (DRM) embedded into the Steam service that they are offering. A better alternative is [GOG.com](https://www.gog.com), and yes it is not completely true ownership of your games but it is certainly a step up because there is no DRM.

## Valve buys influencer bias

Furthermore recently a YouTuber by the name of [Peter Kuchynskyi](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC1jyXqEsbMwu2bkLmwRwpZg) showed the various strategies used by popular "tech" youtubers to show their bias towards products due to them receiving early access, this correlates to Valve due to one of the youtubers being the "Linus Tech Tips" youtube channel receving early access from Valve Corporation to review the Steam machine and in the video the channel downplayed the product's utter expensive cost in order to urge the viewers to make an "investment" as if Valve is some sort of glorious corporation sponsoring Linux, when in reality the product is certainly not worth the money. Spending money on Valve hardware that is not even worth it, to me is equivalent of buying designer brand clothing that isn't even different from a plain white t-shirt.

## Use Steam. But don't worship Valve.

At the same time I am not saying to not use Steam, I am simply pointing out the fact that Valve is not a company that is just pouring money into Linux because they like it.
